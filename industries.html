Industries

Real-world industries. Real impact.

We work where AI, automation, and trade collide. For each sector, we bring the same discipline: sourced strategy, then the system that acts on it. Start with yours.

01

Advanced Manufacturing

Robotics, automation, and the reshoring of production.

Production is being automated and pulled back onshore under a wall of tariffs and deadlines. The winners are reorganizing their factories and supply base around robotics and AI now, not after.

  • Automation and robotics strategy, integration, and deployment.
  • Onshoring and localization of production.
  • Operations, quality, and plant-floor systems.
02

Energy & Resources

The electrification and grid build-out.

Power demand is surging while the hardware that stores and moves it is scarce and foreign-built. Capital is flooding in, and the constraint is speed to a working, financeable system.

  • Power-electronics, storage, and grid-hardware strategy.
  • Localization and supply strategy for scarce components.
  • Electrification and interconnection go-to-market.
03

Infrastructure & Cities

The physical systems of modern life.

Housing shortfalls, aging assets, and new capital programs are colliding with modular construction and smart-infrastructure technology. The opportunity is in building faster and smarter.

  • Capital-project and development strategy.
  • Modular and industrialized building systems.
  • Smart-infrastructure technology deployment.
04

Transportation & Logistics

Supply chains redrawn by trade and automation.

The end of duty-free direct import and a new tariff regime have reset how goods move. Trade strategy and supply-chain design are now a single discipline.

  • Global trade, tariff, and reshoring strategy.
  • Foreign-Trade-Zone and duty optimization design.
  • Logistics-network architecture and systems.
05

Healthcare & Life Sciences

Reshoring molecules, modernizing care.

A heavy dependence on foreign active ingredients and new tariffs are pushing pharma and device supply back onshore, while AI reshapes how care and operations run.

  • Supply-chain, reshoring, and fill-finish strategy.
  • Medical-device kitting and quality systems.
  • Regulatory posture and AI-enabled operations.
06

Agriculture & Food Systems

Traceability and resilient food supply.

New end-to-end traceability rules and a fast-growing cold chain are rewriting how food moves and is recorded, just as automation reaches the field and the facility.

  • Traceability and compliance readiness.
  • Cold-chain and facility network design.
  • Ag-tech and automation deployment, route-to-market.
07

Financial Services

AI-native operations and risk.

AI and agents are rebuilding the cost base, the risk function, and the client experience. The advantage goes to firms that deploy responsibly and at scale, with governance that holds.

  • AI and automation strategy for operations and risk.
  • Operating-model and cost redesign.
  • Deployed systems, controls, and governance.
08

Government & Public Sector

Modernization and resilient supply.

Agencies and public enterprises are modernizing legacy systems, building domestic supply, and adopting AI under real accountability. Strategy has to come with delivery that survives scrutiny.

  • Modernization strategy and delivery.
  • Data and AI systems with clear governance.
  • Domestic-supply, resilience, and workforce strategy.
